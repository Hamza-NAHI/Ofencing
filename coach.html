Meet your coach

From international competition to your first touch.

Omar Nahi is a Moroccan fencer whose competitive weapon is the épée. He has represented Morocco at international events, including the World Championships and the African Championships.

In 2019, he was part of Morocco’s gold-medal-winning men’s épée team at the African Championships in Bamako. He also earned team silver with Morocco at the 2022 edition in Casablanca and the 2023 edition in Cairo.

Alongside his competitive career, Omar has been featured as a fencing coach by Elite Sports Academy. In his L’Grinta interview, he discusses fencing in Morocco.
